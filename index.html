01 / About

          
Thinking like an attacker, building for defenders

        


        

          

            

              I'm a Senior Penetration Tester at BreachLabz, where I lead offensive security engagements across automotive and EV ecosystems: ECUs, in-vehicle networks, EV chargers, backend systems and wireless interfaces, testing them the way real-world attackers would.
            

            

              Alongside hands-on testing, I build in-house pentesting tools to automate workflows and set up our dedicated automotive pentest lab. My background in ISO/SAE 21434, UN R155, TARA, CSMS and fuzz testing, gained helping OEMs and suppliers, lets me turn findings into actionable security controls. I also founded AutoSecurityy, a non-profit community for sharing automotive cybersecurity research and knowledge.
